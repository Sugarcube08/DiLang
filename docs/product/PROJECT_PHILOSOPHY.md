# DiLang Project Philosophy

1. **User Sovereignty & Data Privacy**: Language learning is deeply personal. Your mistakes, interests, and voice recordings belong strictly to you and must never leave your hardware.
2. **Offline-First Reality**: A true tool works everywhere—on airplanes, in remote regions, and during power or internet outages.
3. **Deterministic Memory over Hallucination**: Neural models provide fluid conversation, but memory retention and vocabulary pacing must be anchored by rigorous mathematical algorithms (FSRS v4).
4. **Architectural Permanence**: AI agents and human contributors come and go; clean separation of concerns and immutable governance documents ensure the codebase remains maintainable for decades.
