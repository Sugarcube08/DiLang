# DiLang Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence & Vision
DiLang is an **AI-native**, **privacy-first**, **local-first**, **premium** language learning platform.

### Keywords
- **Calm**: Reduces cognitive anxiety during language practice.
- **Intelligent**: Adaptive, neural, deterministic, precise.
- **Minimal**: Apple Human Interface + Nothing OS simplicity + Linear elegance.
- **Human & Organic**: Soft curves, subtle glassmorphism, readable typography.
- **Long Session Comfort**: Dark background (`#0E1014`), low-contrast dividers, zero harsh glare.

### Anti-Patterns to Avoid
- ❌ Bright saturated neon gradients or cyberpunk tropes.
- ❌ Gamified cartoon aesthetics (no Duolingo or Discord clones).
- ❌ Heavy black borders or retro brutalism.
- ❌ Flat UI defaults with zero depth.

---

## 2. Palette Architecture

### Brand Colors
- **Primary Indigo (`#5E5CE6`)**: Represents knowledge, communication, and trust.
- **Secondary Blue (`#3B82F6`)**: Represents fluidity, logic, and structure.
- **Accent Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Reserved exclusively for mastery, completion, correct speech scores, and success.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`)**: Subtle feedback for minor grammar errors or warnings.
- **Error Red (`#EF4444`)**: Error states and high pronunciation divergence.
- **Information Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**: Contextual hints and linguistic insights.

---

## 3. Logo Concept
The DiLang logo fuses four subtle concepts into an elegant, single-stroke SVG glyph:
1. **Speech Bubble** (Communication)
2. **Globe** (Multilingualism)
3. **Book** (Knowledge)
4. **Neuron** (AI-Native Adaptation)
